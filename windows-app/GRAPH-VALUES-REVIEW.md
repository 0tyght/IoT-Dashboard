# Graph values and conversion inspector — 0.13.0

Overview, motor detail and history reuse one plot component. Each of the three full-width rows now places a 184 px value box to the left of its chart. The box shows all axes, their unit and sample time; cursor movement and keyboard time navigation synchronize all three boxes. Containers below 580 px move the values above the plot. Numerical frequency and temperature remain available separately. Duplicate overview vibration tiles are removed.

The raw/formula inspector opens on hover and can be pinned by click or Enter. Pinned content retains its timestamp while live plots continue; Escape closes it. Changing motors or clearing data dismisses the snapshot. The inspector uses words from that reading, never inferred raw data. It lists zero-based address, decimal/hex word, UInt16 or signed Int16 interpretation and division by 10. Frequency explains Float32 ordering without applying the divisor. Custom readings retain type, byte order, scale, address and unit in decodeConfig in NDJSON; legacy entries do not borrow current settings. Additional tooltips expose conversions for numerical channel cells, frequency and temperature.

Simulation remains explicit. Mock vibration/temperature values now match their 0.1-resolution raw words, while frequency uses Float32 precision. These are presentation and traceability changes; they do not verify hardware frequency order, RMS/Peak semantics, ISO classifications or physical sensor mounting.

Validation covers signed negative acceleration, full-range unsigned displacement, custom formulas and missing metadata; UI checks cover raw-menu hover/pin/Escape/keyboard operation, timestamp retention, full-width equal rows, custom transitions, history and three window sizes. Rendered screenshots inspect the left-hand boxes and actual raw/formula popup. Impeccable's scan of the changed JS/CSS reported no findings; this is not a claim of comprehensive accessibility certification.
