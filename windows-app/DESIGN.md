- Overview and motor detail show three plots in a single vertical column: velocity, displacement and acceleration, each with X/Y/Z and independent units. Every plot spans the available width. Their recorded time-plot equivalent is available in Analysis; History is a table/export workspace. Temperature is numerical only. Frequency X/Y/Z is numerical with its own quality/format explanation; no selector hides measured axes in the monitor.
- Each graph owns a left-hand value box with its axis values, units, sample time and raw/formula inspector. Pointer and keyboard time selection update all three boxes together. Overview no longer duplicates the nine vibration values above charts. In the monitor, three rows divide the remaining height equally and the value boxes stay beside their charts; compact charts reduce tick count without scaling down text. Other chart containers below 580 px can stack values above plots.
- Hover opens raw/formula details; click or Enter pins a timestamped snapshot for reading/copying; Escape closes it. Per-axis hover text also exposes the conversion. Show zero-based addresses, decimal/hex words, signed interpretation, divisor or custom type/order/scale and recorded result. Never reconstruct missing raw data. New custom readings store their decoding configuration alongside NDJSON so history does not apply a later profile's settings. Legacy custom data explicitly reports missing formula metadata. Simulation is identified and quantized consistently with its mock raw words.
- Motor details give most of the viewport to the graphs, with temperature, frequency X/Y/Z and acquisition status in a compact context rail. Full channel data/nameplate opens in a floating panel. Portrait tablet layouts move compact telemetry above full-width plots. A context toggle expands chart width; one/five/thirty-minute shortcuts change the actual time window. Close, Escape and backdrop clicks dismiss it and return focus. Page changes and motor selection close it; widening restores the desktop collapse preference. ## Unified Analysis and History

- Analysis contains two source tabs: **ค่าที่บันทึก · XYZ** and **คลื่นดิบ · FFT**. Default to saved-data analysis, with **กระจาย 3D** selected. Make their source distinction explicit; neither a frequency scalar nor an overall reading is a waveform.
- One scatter point represents one record's complete X/Y/Z values of velocity, displacement or acceleration. All three coordinates share the chosen metric's physical unit. Time uses point color and the timestamp/slider readout. Do not connect the cloud into a surface or trajectory, place motors in it, or label it a histogram, exact density, spectrum or fault classification.
- Provide **แนวโน้มเวลา** alongside the scatter for chronology, original gap provenance and raw/formula inspection. Custom single-register series use this one-value plot rather than manufactured XYZ coordinates. Zero, a missing channel and an empty result are distinct states.
- History holds the table, run-folder access and CSV export. **เปิดกราฟในหน้าวิเคราะห์** opens the saved-data source. Motor and run controls mirror the same selection; chart time fields synchronize their calendar dates with the table filters. The chart may narrow to a time-of-day range while table/export remain calendar-day filters.
- Invalidate graph and table request generations when date/filter state changes. Clear prior rows/curves during replacement loads and on errors. A late response must not place an old motor, run or date under the current controls.
- Scatter data comes from its own backend `scatterRange`, not the time plot's extrema reducer. Filter the recorded series before sampling; cap the UI at 1,200 complete XYZ records. For larger sets, uniform record reservoir sampling retains original values/timestamps and reports sampling, total, incomplete and skipped counts. Preserve the two-pass journal byte limits so ongoing appends cannot change the set between counting and sampling. A sampled cloud is not a claim about the exact density of the full recording.
- In **คลื่นดิบ · FFT**, retain waveform CSV validation, FFT/PSD, colored Surface 3D, Waterfall and Spectrogram. Their axes are frequency, elapsed waveform time and spectral amplitude/density, distinct from the saved XYZ scatter. Keep source labels visible in fullscreen; generated test signals never become production history.

## Overall trend FFT

- Under saved data, **FFT ค่ารวม** uses the same motor, run, recorded-series and timestamp controls as XYZ/time plots. `trendRange` reads every original record in a byte-bounded journal snapshot; no chart reduction or reservoir sample may enter this FFT. A range over 65,536 selected-series records fails with its catalog/count intact.
- Analyze one metric/axis at a time. Velocity/displacement/acceleration, temperature, confirmed frequency values and custom scalar units retain their recorded meaning. Missing axis values, duplicate timestamps and receive gaps over 1.5 times the median interval separate contiguous segments. Default to the longest segment; expose other segments with at least 64 values.
- Default to explicitly labelled linear time adjustment, limited to 20% interval jitter inside a segment. A strict option permits at most 0.1% interval jitter. Neither mode crosses a missing-data gap or changes stored records. Fs is the selected segment's count/span receive rate; it is not a claim about the sensor's internal update rate, RMS window or anti-alias filter.
- Keep the time series and FFT/PSD readable in the default 2D view. A labelled button switches to the time-frequency Surface/Waterfall/Spectrogram using the same prepared trend series. Fullscreen retains **FFT ค่ารวม · ไม่ใช่ FFT คลื่นสั่นสะเทือน** provenance. Show peak-cycle duration in seconds, never convert it to motor RPM.
- Waveform and trend analyzers use separate DOM IDs, workers and input state while sharing numerical/plot implementations. Changing filters clears pending trend work and prior results. Units, selected timestamps, timing method, jitter, analyzed/excluded records, FFT size, window, actual sample rate and omitted tail samples remain inspectable. No imported or computed trend is written as a sensor waveform.
